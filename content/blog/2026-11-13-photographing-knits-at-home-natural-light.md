When people shop online, they can't touch the softness of a scarf or see how a jumper drapes. Your photos do that job for them. The lovely news is that good product pictures don't need fancy gear. A modern phone and some gentle daylight are plenty.

## Find your best window

Soft, indirect light is the knitter's friend. Strong direct sun tends to wash out colour and throw hard shadows across your stitches, and overhead room lights can add a yellow tinge. Set up beside a large window so the light falls across your piece from the side, never from behind it. That side light picks out every cable and rib. Cloudy days are a gift here, because the clouds soften everything for you. Skip the phone's flash, which flattens texture and can't really be fixed later.

## Keep the setup simple

- **Background:** a plain sheet, a linen cloth or a painted board works well. Taping fabric to the wall and letting it curve down onto the table hides the seam where they meet.
- **Steady phone:** a small tripod, or the phone propped on a pile of books with the timer on, stops blurry shots.
- **Get close:** move the phone nearer rather than zooming in, and tap the screen to focus on the knit.
- **Few props:** a ball of yarn or a sprig of greenery can add warmth, as long as the knitting stays the star.

## Shoot the shots shoppers need

Aim for a small set for each piece: the whole item, a close-up of the stitch texture, and one showing how it hangs or is worn, on a hanger, a dress form or a person. Take several photos of each and pick the best later. Using the same spot and background every time makes your whole shop look calm and connected.

## Make it fit family life

Good light comes at set times, so it helps to plan around it. Gathering a few finished pieces and photographing them together in one session, perhaps on a quiet morning, is easier than dashing to the window for every single order. Leave the setup folded in a corner and it's ready next time. Half an hour every week or two is often enough, and the rest of the day stays yours.

Happy snapping, from all of us here at Bella Summer Rose, where every piece is knitted by hand.
