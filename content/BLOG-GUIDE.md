# Blog writing guide: Bella Summer Rose Designs

Bella Summer Rose Designs is Heleen Rose's hand-knitting shop (South Africa, ships worldwide). Its look and voice are warm, earthy, cosy and boho. The blog gives visitors something useful and kind to read, and gives them a reason to come back and browse the shop.

## Where posts go
- One file per post: `content/blog/YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug.md` (today's date, lowercase slug with hyphens).
- The file starts with this front matter, then the post in Markdown:

```
---
title: "A clear, friendly title (under 70 characters)"
date: YYYY-MM-DD
summary: "One sentence that makes someone want to read it (under 160 characters)."
category: Techniques
---
```

- `category` must be one of: **Techniques**, **New ideas**, **Yarn & tools**, **Home business**, **Gifts & seasons**.
- Supported Markdown: paragraphs, `##` / `###` headings, `-` bullets, `1.` numbered lists, `> quotes`, **bold**, *italic*, `[links](https://…)`. No images, HTML or tables.

## Rotation
- **Monday:** Techniques (a stitch, a fix, a finishing trick, a skill for beginners).
- **Wednesday:** New ideas or Yarn & tools (a trend, a new kind of yarn or tool, a project idea, a seasonal make).
- **Friday:** Home business (mums building small businesses from home: time, pricing handmade work, photographing products, selling online, keeping a happy balance with family). Use Gifts & seasons instead when a holiday is near.
- Never repeat a topic already covered in `content/blog/`. Check the existing titles first.

## How to write
- **Original, always.** Read several sources to understand the topic, then close them and write in your own words. Never copy or lightly reword sentences, never reproduce someone's pattern, and never quote more than a few words. Facts and ideas are fine to share; other people's wording, photos and patterns are not.
- **Short and sweet:** 300 to 450 words. One practical idea per post, explained clearly.
- **Voice:** warm, encouraging and down-to-earth, like a friend who knits. Plain words, short paragraphs. Use South African/British spelling (colour, favourite, mum).
- **Structure:** a short opening, two or three `##` sections or a short step list, and a one-line friendly close. When it fits naturally, end with a gentle mention that every piece in the shop is knitted by hand. No hard selling.
- **Honesty:** write as the shop ("we", "here at Bella Summer Rose"). Do not invent personal stories, quotes, customers, statistics or experiences for Heleen. Only state facts you found in reliable sources. Do not give medical or financial advice. For business topics, keep it practical and general.
- **Kind and inclusive:** for home-business posts, write for any parent or carer juggling family and work. No guilt, no stereotypes, no "hustle" pressure. Balance and rest are part of the advice.
- **Further reading:** finish with a `## Further reading` section listing 1 to 3 sources you actually used, as `[Site or article name](https://…)`. Use real pages from your research only.
- **Safe and timeless:** no news about people, no prices that go out of date quickly, nothing political, and nothing about specific brands unless it is a genuinely new product worth knowing about.
